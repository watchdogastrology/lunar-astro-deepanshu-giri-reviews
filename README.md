### 1. The "Bait-and-Switch" Consultation Funnel
Lunar Astro charges premium rates (₹5,000+) for consultations, heavily utilizing Deepanshu Giri's image in advertisements. However, post-payment, clients are routinely assigned to junior "proxy students" instead of Mr. Giri. 
* **The Violation:** Advertising a specific expert but delivering a junior trainee constitutes an Unfair Trade Practice under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
* **The Aftermath:** Clients requesting refunds for undelivered services are routinely denied, offered only 50%, or blocked entirely.

### 2. Intellectual Property Theft Allegations
Lunar Astro markets a premium predictive technique called *Paribhraman Paddhati*. Evidence and testimonies from former associates indicate this is a rebranded version of the *BCP (Bhrigu Chakra Paddhati)* technique, originally taught by Sunil John of Saptarishis Astrology, under whom Deepanshu Giri previously studied.

### 3. Digital Harassment & Fake Reviews
To maintain a 4.9-star rating, Lunar Astro allegedly utilizes its student base as a troll farm. 
* **Fake Positives:** Students are instructed to leave 5-star reviews to bury legitimate complaints.
* **Intimidation:** Consumers who leave 1-star reviews report receiving threatening messages from Lunar Astro affiliates, falsely claiming that "Cyber Cell police" are tracking their IP addresses for defamation.

### 4. Consumer Action Guide
If you have been a victim of the Lunar Astro bait-and-switch, follow these steps:
1. **Initiate a Chargeback:** Contact your bank/credit card provider immediately. State that the transaction was fraudulent because the "service was not provided as described/advertised."
2. **File with NCH:** Register a formal complaint with the National Consumer Helpline (INGRAM) by calling 1915.
3. **Report Threats:** If you receive "Cyber Cell" threats for leaving a review, screenshot them and report the intimidation to cybercrime.gov.in.
